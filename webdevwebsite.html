Burst Safely

A good celebration is a safe celebration.

✅ Do

  1. Light crackers outdoors in an open area.
  2. Keep a bucket of water nearby.
  3. Wear cotton clothes and footwear.
  4. Light one cracker at a time using a long incense stick.
  5. Keep children under adult supervision.

❌ Don't

  • Never relight a cracker that did not burst.
  • Never hold a cracker in your hand while lighting.
  • Never burst crackers near petrol or gas.
  • Never wear loose synthetic clothes.
  • Never throw crackers at people or animals.
